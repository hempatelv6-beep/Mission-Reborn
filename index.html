    How to use Mission Reborn

    1. Pick a world. Choose the Moon or Mars. Each has five machines.
    2. Scroll down. A small planet slides along the line to show how far you've read. Tap a dotted word to see what it means.
    3. Take the quizzes. Each machine has a short quiz. Finish both worlds to unlock the Final Mission.

    Too much movement? Turn on Calm mode in the top corner.
